# Stakeholders — Health Camp Analytics Platform

## 1. Camp Operations Head
Owns the day-to-day running of health-checkup camps across Maharashtra — scheduling,
staffing, vendor coordination. Before this platform, camp performance data sat in
scattered vendor Excel files with no single view.

Pain point: "I can't tell which camps ran at a loss or under-delivered until weeks
later, by which point it's too late to fix anything for that camp."

## 2. Marketing / Client Relations Lead
Owns renewal conversations with corporate clients (the companies who pay for employee
health camps). Needs company-wise health trend data to make the renewal pitch and to
flag at-risk accounts.

Pain point: "I find out a client's employee health metrics are declining only when
they bring it up in a renewal meeting — I have no early warning system."

## 3. Finance Lead
Tracks revenue, cost, and margin per camp across all 3 vendors. Needs consistent,
accurate numbers regardless of which vendor ran the camp.

Pain point: "Each vendor sends cost and headcount data in a different format, so I
spend days every month just reconciling numbers before I can report actual margin."

## 4. Clinical / Medical Lead
Reviews aggregate health trends (BP, sugar, BMI) across camps to flag populations at
risk and to advise corporate clients on wellness interventions.

Pain point: "I don't have visibility into which readings are actually reliable — bad
sensor data and vendor-entry errors mix in with real readings and I can't tell them
apart at a glance."

## 5. Operations / Logistics Manager
Owns the medical equipment (BP monitors, ECG machines, X-ray units, glucometers) used
across camps, including maintenance scheduling.

Pain point: "There's no central alert for devices coming due for service — we've had
equipment go into a live camp overdue for calibration because no one tracked it."
